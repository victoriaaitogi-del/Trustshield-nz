Global Features Built In From Day One
🛂
Global Trust Passport
A Gold-rated NZ business carries their TrustShield rating to partner platforms in AU, UK, Canada and beyond — like an international trust licence.
🔗
Consumer Safety API
Banks, telcos, and retailers plug TrustShield data in. When you're about to pay an unverified business, your bank app shows a warning before you proceed.
🚨
Real-Time Scam Alerts
Like an Amber Alert for scams. When a threat is confirmed, every registered user and subscribed school is notified instantly — by region and scam type.
🏡
Whānau Safety Mode
A uniquely NZ-designed family safety network. Resonates globally in cultures with strong family structures — Pacific, Asian, South American communities.
⛓️
Blockchain Verified Reviews
Every review is cryptographically timestamped. Nobody — not even TrustShield — can alter a review retroactively. Fully auditable by anyone.

Multilingual by Design
Launched in English and Te Reo Māori. Expanding to Samoan, Tongan, Hindi, Mandarin — then globally. Not a Western-centric platform.
🏫
Curriculum Aligned
School modules meeting NZ MoE digital citizenship standards. Once NZ-recognised, approaching education ministries in AU, UK, CA is straightforward.
👥
Community Reviewer Network
Trained, accredited community members — teachers, librarians, retirees — become certified TrustShield Reviewers, earning a small fee per verified review.
